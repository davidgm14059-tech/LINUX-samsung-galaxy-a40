# Unlocking bootloader
## IMPORTANT 🛑
this will destroy ALL of your data on the phone, do a backup before procedding
## 1. enabling oem unlocking
1. open settings
2. go to about phone
3. find the option that says "Build number"
4. tap it 7 times
5. go back
6. tap in "Developer options"
7. search for "OEM unlocking" and enable it
8. reboot your phone in download mode
